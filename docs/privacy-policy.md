# Sumi privacy policy

*Last updated: 5 November 2026*

Sumi is a Sudoku game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: **Sumi collects nothing, works entirely offline and has no ads.**

## What Sumi collects

Nothing. Sumi has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. It does not ask for any Android permissions, including internet access, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

## What Sumi stores on your phone

To let you carry on where you left off, Sumi keeps these on your device, in storage only Sumi can read:

- your game in progress and your daily puzzle in progress
- your settings, such as theme and vibration
- your statistics, daily puzzle results and achievements
- which lessons you have finished

If you add Sumi's home-screen widget, it shows today's daily puzzle, your streak and this week's solved days from the same stored information. This information never leaves your phone through Sumi. Puzzles, including the daily puzzle, are made on your phone, so no network is needed.

## Backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup or phone-to-phone transfer, Android includes the information above in that backup, so it comes with you to a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account, not by Sumi, and you control it in your phone's settings.

You can also save a backup file yourself, in Settings › Your progress › Save a backup file. Sumi writes it only to the place you pick in Android's file picker, and it contains only the information listed above. What happens to it after that is up to you, for example if you copy it to cloud storage. Restore from a backup reads a file you pick and replaces what is on the phone.

## Deleting your information

Uninstalling Sumi deletes everything it stored. You can also clear it at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Sumi › Storage › Clear storage. To reset only your statistics, use Reset on Sumi's Progress screen. Backup files you saved are yours to delete.

## Children

Sumi is suitable for all ages. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Any change that adds data collection would be described before it takes effect.
